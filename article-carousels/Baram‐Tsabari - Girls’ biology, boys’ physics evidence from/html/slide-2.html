02/03
  • The authors analysed 1,169 spontaneous questions about biology and physics sent to online and TV science services.
  • Biology made up 53.8% of females’ questions and 38.3% of males’; physics, 6% versus 13.2%.
  • The gap widened with age, as girls seemed to develop less interest in physics than boys did.

Baram‐Tsabari and Yarden (2008) · Research in Science & Technological Education · doi.org/10.1080/02635140701847538
