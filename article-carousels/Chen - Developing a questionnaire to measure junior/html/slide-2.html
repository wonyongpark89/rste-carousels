  • Science capital describes students’ science-⁠related resources, but online learning adds conditions such as device access and digital participation.
  • The authors developed a questionnaire on online science capital from Bourdieu’s theory and Archer et al.’s framework.
  • It was completed by 364 Taiwanese junior high school students alongside two aspiration items.
