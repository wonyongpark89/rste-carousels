  • The analysis revealed four distinct instructional profiles.
  • About two-⁠thirds of teachers reported combining inquiry-⁠based strategies, yet only one profile reflected a comprehensive inquiry approach, with students engaged throughout the inquiry cycle.
  • Profile membership was significantly associated with gender and professional development; the authors call for stronger reflective competencies in teacher education.
