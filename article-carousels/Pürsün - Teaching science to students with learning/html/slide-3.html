  • Students learned about foods within the food groups they encountered in everyday life.
  • They showed conceptual deficiencies in relating nutrients to everyday foods and struggled with science terminology.
  • Despite teachers’ reported use of everyday examples, students still struggled to link them to scientific concepts.
  • Teachers’ use of instructional adaptations showed deficiencies.
