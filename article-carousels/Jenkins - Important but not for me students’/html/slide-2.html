02/03
  • Drawing on the international Relevance of Science Education (ROSE) project, Jenkins and Nelson analysed responses from 1,266 students in England.
  • 68% agreed everyone should learn science at school and 61% found it interesting, yet only 21% wanted to become scientists.
  • The article helped bring students’ perspectives into debates on science curriculum reform.
