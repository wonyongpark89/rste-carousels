# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS slides (1080 × 1350 px each), rendered to PNG and PDF with headless Chrome. Fonts are open-licence files stored locally under `assets/fonts`.

## Users

Science education researchers, teachers, teacher educators and prospective authors scrolling LinkedIn on a phone or desktop. They see the carousel among other posts and decide in a second or two whether to swipe.

## Product Purpose

A seven-slide LinkedIn carousel introducing the editorial team of *Research in Science & Technological Education* (RSTE, Routledge / Taylor & Francis). Success: readers recognise the journal, know who its editors are and what they research, and know where to read the journal and find author guidance.

## Positioning

RSTE is an established international journal; its people are a genuinely international team (UK, Hong Kong, China, USA, Singapore, Spain). The carousel shows real people, real affiliations and real research interests, nothing more.

## Operating Context

Posted mainly on LinkedIn as a document carousel (PDF matters most), with PNGs for other uses. Viewed largely at phone width, so a 1080 px slide is seen at roughly 360–400 px.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Seven slides, exact wording supplied by the Editor (Wonyong Park) on 16 October 2026; UK English.
- Names, roles, affiliations and research phrases must be reproduced exactly. No biographies, quotations, titles, metrics or claims.
- Source references live in `SOURCES.md`, never on the artwork.
- Portraits are real institutional photos; no generated or reconstructed faces, no retouching or background replacement.
- Low-resolution sources: Chris Harrison (160 × 160), Soo Won Shim (338 × 336, soft). Moderate: Jack Pun (402 × 480), Ezgi Yesilyurt (400 × 500), Radu Bogdan Toma (500 × 500).

## Brand Commitments

- Colour: orange, black and white, taken from the current RSTE issue cover (`photos/crst20.v044.i03.largecover.jpg`, sampled orange #F6892A; black title type). Do not reproduce the cover's volume, number, date, ISSN or index badge.
- No official logo supplied; do not recreate the Routledge mark.
- Avoid gradients, glow, decorative science icons, stock illustrations, rounded cards, badges/pills, unnecessary ornament and promotional language.

## Evidence on Hand

- Source notes: `Downloads/RSTE_Editors_Photos_and_Claude_Prompt/rste_carousel_assets/README_SOURCES.txt`.

## Product Principles

1. The people are the content; design serves recognition and readability.
2. Equal treatment signals a collegial team: paired editors share prominence; associate editors share identical treatment.
3. Accuracy over flourish: every word is supplied, nothing is embellished.
4. Readable on a phone first.
